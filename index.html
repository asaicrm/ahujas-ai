How these get built.

Three rules that hold across everything with our name on it.

Rule one A deterministic core

Anything that can be calculated is calculated. Models explain the result and put it in context — they never invent the number underneath it.

Rule two Privacy by construction

The less we keep, the less there is to lose. Data stays in the United States, conversations aren't retained, and nothing is sold. It's an architecture decision, not a policy page.

Rule three One short path

Founder-built, full stack, sixteen years of enterprise engineering behind it. An idea reaches production in days and a fix reaches you in minutes.
