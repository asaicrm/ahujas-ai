How we work.

The shape varies with the engagement. The rhythm is consistent.

Scope

A working session on what the system has to do, what it must never do, and what success looks like. That becomes the SOW.

Build

Agent teams write, test, and review in parallel, against specs your engineer writes and merges they approve.

Review

You work against a running environment, not a status deck. Feedback goes in and comes back quickly.

Continue

Enhancements, maintenance, the next phase, or a clean exit — whichever the SOW calls for.

Who is accountable.

"AI built it" is not a quality standard. This is what stands behind the work.

Responsibility Your engineer signs off

Every merge is reviewed and approved by the forward deployed engineer on your engagement. Agents do the labor. The judgment is human, and it has a name attached to it.

Verification Tested against the spec

Automated tests, dependency and vulnerability scanning, and acceptance criteria written into the SOW. You see results, not assurances.

Transparency Readable, conventional code

Standard frameworks, documented decisions, no proprietary runtime of ours in the middle of your stack. Another engineer can read it and keep going.
